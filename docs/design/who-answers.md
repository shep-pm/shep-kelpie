# Who answers a ruling

Who answers a ruling: the maintainer, or the project manager for the kinds `rulings.pm_decides` gives it. The App's posts are in `github-app.md`, and the panel's history in `docs/facts/the-panel.md` (ADR 0018).

- `rulings.pm_decides`: map of every kind, `true` = PM answers, `false` or absent = maintainer. Defaults in `RulingName::pm_default`: `merge`, `question`, `foreign-change`, `agent-files`, `agent-fit` and `over-budget` `false`, the rest `true`. `question`'s value decides nothing: `pm_answers_ruling` leaves a question and a follow-up out as `own_wake` and `pm_asked` wakes the PM for each whatever the map says (pm.md, #1808). A follow-up's `follow_up` key decides each finding on its own: a yes may `drop` findings by number, each with its why, and files the rest, and every one dropped is a no; the maintainer's yes or no stays all or nothing (#1809). `true` refused for `security` and `contribution-merge` (a stranger's text must not reach an agent that answers it) and `local-model-spilled`. `merge` may be `true`, the one key for unattended merging (rulings.md); a warned head still goes to the maintainer. `over-budget` `false` still wakes the PM, to recommend: no setting lifts the spend cap (ADR 0020); `true` has it answer (#1792). `add` and `settings migrate` (by `upgrade`) write missing kinds at their default, never an existing key; old `pm_alone` loads one release as `true`. ADR 0018, #466, #1761, #1762, `crates/core/src/settings/rulings.rs`
- `agent-fit` (ADR 0018, #1546): after each `finished` line naming an agent and a kind, the runner asks `AgentBoard::fit` about that pair, and a finding raises the ruling on that item's pull request (agent-board-runner.md). It parks no work item, as a contribution's does, and a ruling already waiting under that number keeps its place. A yes and a no both change no file: kelpie never writes an agent's `kinds`, so an answer only clears the ruling, and the dog marks the pair so it answers the same one again only after `FIT_AGAIN` more items. `runner/agent_fit.rs`, `runner/ledger.rs`, `core/state/ruling.rs`
- A ruling of a `true` kind with 2+ options, with `agents.pm` named, wakes the PM with `Wake::Answer` (`pm_asked`, on the save that raises it), and its comment waits on that one wake (`pm.asking`, `Ruling.unposted`). Every other ruling posts at once. `runner/pm/rulings.rs`
- Never the PM's: a contribution's ruling, a turn kelpie could not set up (`TurnFailed { setup: true }`, the fix is on this machine), a ruling raised in a drain, and a `turn-timeout` or `turn-failed` ruling past `STALLED_RESUMES` at its head (`turn_stalled`), which posts at once with `pm decide` and an `unstick` `retry` refused and its card saying why (rulings.md). It still wakes the PM as a stuck item, and `fix` and `ask` are its way to act there. #1250, #1368, #1810, #1821
- The PM parks a stuck item on a fix already open with the `fix` key (`{item, on, why}`, one or a list, no limit on how many land on one fix). `settle_fix` reads `on` with `Tickets::issue`, and it settles the item's ruling with `Action::Ask` under the lead `ruling::PARKS`, so the item stays parked. The card reads that lead back (`voice/card/mod.rs`, `pm_words`) and shows the fix with or without the `why`. The card shows the PM's latest word only: a `pm recommend` pick written after a park replaces the park line, and a park after a pick replaces that. A `fix` naming a closed issue, the item itself, or an item not stuck on a ruling is dropped and named. `fix` also drafts the issue, `{item, title, body, why}` for `on` (#1812): `checked` runs `names_machine` and `board::check::check` with a stand-in `priority: P0` and `kind:fix`, the check a red fix's body gets too, and a failing draft goes to the maintainer, no call made. A clean one is read by `pm_fix_read_call` before any wake: `pm-fix-read` under the worker folder cleared first, the checkout the only code, nothing to write, no host, `Tools::Review`, a new session saved nowhere, on `agents.pm`'s model. The queue is in memory, so a restart loses a waiting draft. A pass files `ready-for-agent`, `priority: P1` and `kind:fix` first in `state.order`, and parks the item on it; a refusal, a failed call, timeout or no verdict files nothing and settles with `Action::Ask` carrying the why and, under it, the draft whole in `card::draft_block` delimiter lines, which the card (`drafted`) and the webhook alert both carry. `runner/pm/fix_read.rs` #1810, `runner/pm/fix.rs`, `runner/pm/act.rs`
- The answer is the `votes` key, one `{"ruling", "choice", "why", "design"}` each (`crates/core/src/pm_answer/`). A choice that is an option is applied through `Settler::Pm` and posted headed "Answered by the project manager: A, <option>"; `<id> no <note>` there reopens it within 24 h while its work item is open and its worker not running (`threads.decided`, `watch_decided`). #466
- A timed-out or failed turn takes three answers by `votes` option: `A` resumes; `A` with `"ask": true` resumes after a fixed note, `PROGRESS_FIRST` (`ruling/prompts.rs`, `ask_progress_first`), asking what the worker has done so far, none for a first turn with no session; `B` parks. `pm_decides` refuses the no there, since stopping a work item is the maintainer's, so the ruling goes to them with the pick on its card and the item stays in `Phase::Ruling`. They hear of a park only. `ask` on another kind is ignored and logged; `unstick` `retry` stays a plain resume. #1820
- To the maintainer instead, with `**Project manager: A**. <why>` on the card, or by name where its table names its options (`**Project manager: Merge**`): a `design` flag, a merge yes on a warned head, a pick only the maintainer can carry out (`Ask the maintainer`, an option that begins "You"), a pick that is no option, or an answer that cannot be applied. No answer, or a wake that failed, posts the ruling as it stands. The pick is kept in the ruling's question as `PM recommends:` / `PM says:` lines.
- A ruling left to the maintainer wakes no PM until they reply in its thread (`runner/pm/asked.rs`, `Wake::Answer { request }`, `pm.asking`): `<id> pm recommend` posts its pick there as `**Project manager: A**` with the reply that gives it, still theirs; `<id> pm options` posts up to 5 other approaches, each `inert`, kept as `Ruling.approaches` (state file 88), and `<id> pm <n>` takes one as their `no <note>`, or a question's answer, raw; `<id> pm decide` hands it over as if its kind were `true`, refused for the kinds `true` is refused for and the never-the-PM's above. No `agents.pm`: all refused. A second request while its wake runs is answered once; a failed wake says so in the thread. ADR 0018
- Free text under a ruling, and the `said` key the PM answers it with: [pm-words.md](pm-words.md)
- Question listing under 2 options: the PM's question wake drafts 2-3 `candidates` (one may be `Ask the maintainer`), which become its `- ` options for the maintainer. No PM = no candidates. #709, `runner/pm/question.rs`
- An option can name an action kelpie runs in code when chosen (rerun, restore). See merge.md. #710
- An answer, or a ruling's note, is the worker's next turn. A resume its harness finds no session for (`NoSession`) starts over once with the same id: the item's brief, then what the lost resume was to send. `runner/turn/mod.rs`
- Each answer is a line of `rulings.jsonl` (`runner/ruling/history.rs`), by the maintainer or the PM; `status` counts the PM's answers by kind (`pm_answers`). An older line reads; one the panel decided is skipped.
- What the panel left (ADR 0018): the state file (87) drops a ruling's `panel`, `verdict` and `skipped` on load. Old `vote` ledger lines still read (`LedgerKind::Retired`). `settings migrate` deletes `rulings.decide`, `rulings.full_panel_paths`, `agents.advisor`, `agents.researcher`, the `researcher` table and `repo_setup.roles`, all refused by name (`src/flock/set/migrate.rs`). `Role::Advisor` and `Role::Researcher` are gone with their tools, agent files and baselines (`agents migrate` deletes a stub holding only its binding); their ledger lines count on one `retired roles` line of `shep kelpie usage` (`LedgerRole::Retired`).
