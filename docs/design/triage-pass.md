# The triage pass

One issue's pass (#887): the call, the post, the labels, the duplicate close, the agent-ready branch for a maintainer issue the pass is sure of (#891), and the wait on an author it is not sure about (#892). The settings and pick rule are in `triage.md`, the author's round in `triage-author.md`, the issue's images in `triage-images.md`.

- `Runner::triage_due` runs from `dispatch` after `contribution_due` and before the board's pick, so it needs the board open and a free slot. It takes the issue the verb queued, then the oldest in `triage.due`, one at a time, and starts nothing while draining or where no App covers the repo. Each issue is tried once a poll. The poll's forge reads are made before the pass takes the lock (`runner-lock.md`). `runner/triage/`
- Before an issue's first call its comments are read once (`Tickets::issue_comments`). A comment by the App's login holding `<!-- kelpie:triage -->` records the issue with no call, and one holding `<!-- kelpie:triage duplicate #n -->` closes it as a duplicate of `n`. Only the last line's marker counts: worded text can hold marker-shaped text of its own. The marker in anyone else's comment counts for nothing. The verb's issue skips the first read, and is read past its old record's comment. The call's ledger line is keyed apart from any work item's (`flight/triage.rs` `KEY`).
- The call is a flight: `runner/flight/triage.rs`, one at a time on its own thread, `CEILING` 15 minutes, `News::Triaged`. `slot_issues` counts it and a drain lists it in `calls` as role `triager`. It runs `triage.agent` in a `worktree::view` of `origin/main` at `<project>/triage/<n>/`, removed after, with no repo plugins.
- The prompt holds the issue and the open issues, all `fenced`. Code keeps a related number only if the listing holds it, a file only if the view holds it, cuts `open` at `max_questions`, and turns a `P0` into an open question. `runner/triage/research.rs`
- The representative words the facts (`Runner::represent_issue`), code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it. Then the type the repo has (`Forge::repo_labels`), left off if missing, and the priority under the board's name `priority: P2`, or the bare `P2` on a repo that names them so (`board::repo_priority`), made under the board's name where neither exists. Then `needs-triage`, made if missing. `runner/triage/comment.rs`
- `triage.issues` holds `{issue, comment, step, labels, duplicate_of, ready_body, seen, questions, local}`, `step` being `commented`, `closed`, `labelled`, `body-written`, `answers`, `ready` or `done`, `labels` the type and priority still owed, `duplicate_of` the item a closed duplicate names, `ready_body` the body owed over the issue's or under its author's text, `seen` the labels the issue carried when its pass began, and `questions` and `local` while the pass waits on its author, each left out while empty. The post is the one write with no save before it: the marker, read again after every post, catches a post whose record did not save. Each later write follows a save of its step. The last 200 `done` records are kept (`TRIAGED_KEPT`), keeping every record that closed a duplicate: it carries no label and keeps a reopened one out of `triage.due`. A poll finishes a record short of `done` or `ready`.
- A failure is told once in the log and tried at the next poll. Five give the issue up until a restart. A busy forge does not count. A research that could not be posted is kept in memory and posted at the next poll.
- The ledger line is role `triager`, kind `triage`, with the issue. A body edit is its own line, kind `edit-issue`. `crates/core/src/forge_ledger/kind.rs`

## A duplicate

- The research also answers whether something covers it. The prompt carries the open pull requests beside the open issues, `fenced` and without the issue itself: number, title, and the issues its body names with `Resolves`, `Fixes` or `Closes`, from `Forge::open_pulls`: the keyword is a word of its own, takes a colon before the number and markup before itself, and a number ends at whitespace or punctuation. The reply gains `addressed_by`: null or `{number, kind, why}`, `kind` being `issue` or `pull_request`. `runner/triage/`, `adapters/gh/issues.rs`, `agents/triager.md`
- Code keeps it only when its number is one the call was shown, the forge still lists it as open and of the kind it was given as, and it is not the issue itself. What it cannot keep (a `kind` that is neither word included) is dropped and told in the log, and the issue is triaged as any other. An unreadable listing fails the pass. The cover is read again before the post, since wording can take minutes: a cover that went is not posted, and the issue is triaged at the next poll. `runner/triage/addressed.rs`
- A kept one ends the pass: the representative words a short comment naming the other item as `#n` and the reason, one plain thanks naming their report; code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it, the record saves `closed`, and only then `Tickets::close_issue_as(repo, ticket, IssueCloseReason)` closes it for reason `duplicate`. `close_issue` is unchanged; no label goes on a closed duplicate. `runner/triage/comment.rs`, `crates/core/src/ports/tickets.rs`, `crates/core/src/ports/issue.rs`
- A restart at `closed` closes it and posts nothing again. `status` says `closed as a duplicate of #n` once the close is done and `to close as a duplicate of #n` while it is owed. An author who reopens one is triaged again only through `shep kelpie triage <n>`.

## The maintainer's issue, made agent-ready

- Sure means a type, a priority, no open question and a `body` `board::has_criteria` accepts that names `Touches:`. Only an issue whose author is `Runner::maintainer()`, ignoring case, is made ready: an outside issue never does. `runner/triage/ready.rs`
- The comment is worded as any other, its facts saying kelpie will rewrite the body and label it `ready-for-agent`. The promise is made only where kelpie can keep it: not while a hand triage label stands, and not for a body the local guard would refuse. `runner/triage/comment.rs` (`facts`), `ready.rs` (`readiness`)
- `Voice::edit_issue_body` replaces the issue's body, the title untouched: `AppVoice` sends the REST issue edit, `PATCH /repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/{number}` naming only `body`, through `GuardedVoice`, `PacedVoice`, `RecordedVoice`. A body naming a path on this machine is dropped before the comment is worded, on the `LocalPaths` check every App post passes, and the issue ends at `needs-triage` with its body as written. A refusal kelpie cannot read beforehand, a `publish` hook, is said on the issue in a short notice, since the comment promised the rewrite. `crates/core/src/ports/voice.rs`, `github/voice/`, `github/api.rs`, `crates/core/src/ports/local_paths/mod.rs`
- After the comment comes the body edit (`body-written`), then the type and priority the repo has, then the `needs-triage` an earlier pass left off, then `ready-for-agent` last (`ready`), made if the repo lacks it: an issue is not both ready for the board and waiting for the maintainer. The `labelled` step is unused here. `runner/triage/ready.rs`
- A refused edit is told once and tried at the next poll, under the five-failure give-up. Once `body-written` is saved the body is never written again: a restart at `commented` holds it in `ready_body`, and a restart at `body-written` or `ready` labels, or does nothing.
- A triage label put on by hand stands, as `runner/flight/label/` does for `agent:` labels. The labels the issue carried when its pass began are read against the issue before the comment is worded, before the body write and before the labelling, so one that came by at any point ends the pass at `done` with the body kept and nothing more labelled. That reading is the record's `seen`, which survives a restart. At the labelling step `ready-for-agent` is the label kelpie is writing, so on the issue there it is kelpie's own and the step finishes; any other triage label there stands. `runner/triage/ready.rs`
- `ready` counts as finished: pruned like `done`, no new pass. The board takes it as any `ready-for-agent` issue, its `agent:` label as ever.
