# The triage pass

One issue's pass. Settings: `triage.md`; the check that owes it: `triage-check.md`; answers: `triage-author.md`; images: `triage-images.md`.

- `Runner::triage_due` runs after `contribution_due` and before the board pick, with the board open, a free slot, an App and no drain. It takes a queued issue before the oldest in `triage.due`, one per poll. Forge reads precede the lock (`runner-lock.md`). `runner/triage/`
- Before the first call, `Tickets::issue_comments` reads for the App's last-line `<!-- kelpie:triage -->` marker: it records the issue without a call. `<!-- kelpie:triage duplicate #n -->` also closes it. Other authors' markers count for nothing. The verb reads past its old comment. `flight/triage.rs` uses its own ledger `KEY`.
- The call is a flight: `runner/flight/triage.rs`, one at a time on its thread, `CEILING` 15 minutes, `News::Triaged`. `slot_issues` counts it and a drain lists it in `calls` as role `triager`. It runs `triage.agent` in a `worktree::view` of `origin/main` at `<project>/triage/<n>/`, removed after, with no repo plugins.
- The prompt holds the issue, open issues and earlier rounds, `fenced`. Code checks related numbers against the listing and files against the view, caps `open` at `max_questions`, and asks about `P0`. Before wording, `scrub.rs` checks questions (including recommendations), files, tests and criteria with `LocalPaths::find` on `Surface::Prose`. A refused part is left out of the facts, questions and terminal prompt. The prompt also omits a refused title. Logs name only counts and `Leak`, never the text. `/home/me` is a placeholder and passes. `runner/triage/{research,scrub,local}.rs`
- The representative words the facts (`Runner::represent_issue`), code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it. Then the type the repo has (`Forge::repo_labels`), left off if missing, and the priority under the board's name `priority: P2`, or the bare `P2` on a repo that names them so (`board::repo_priority`), made under the board's name where neither exists, and `kind:<type>` (`rewrite.rs`). Then `needs-triage`, made if missing. `runner/triage/comment.rs`
- `triage.issues` holds `{issue, comment, step, labels, duplicate_of, ready_body, seen, begun, questions, local}`, `step` being `commented`, `closed`, `labelled`, `body-written`, `answers`, `ready` or `done`, `labels` the type and priority still owed, `duplicate_of` the item a closed duplicate names, `ready_body` the body owed over the issue's or under its author's text, `seen` the labels at its pass's start or its record, and `questions` and `local` while the pass waits on its author, each timed from its comment and left out while empty. The post is the one write with no save before it: the marker, read again after every post, catches a post whose record did not save, and its record asks the comment what it owes. Each later write follows a save of its step. The last 200 `done` records are kept (`TRIAGED_KEPT`), keeping every record that closed a duplicate: it carries no label and keeps a reopened one out of `triage.due`. A poll finishes a record short of `done` or `ready`.
- A failure is logged once and retried next poll. Five give up until restart; a busy forge does not count. A reply that does not parse gets one repair. Research survives a failed post. Wording naming this machine gets one more call with facts saying the last wording named `<leak>` and to leave it out; a second refusal counts as a failure. A forge refusal keeps its `Leak` for the next wording call. Clean wording clears the reason. Repeating any refused body counts as a failure and drops it without posting. `runner/triage/scrub.rs`
- The ledger line is role `triager`, kind `triage`, with the issue. A body edit is its own line, kind `edit-issue`. `crates/core/src/forge_ledger/kind.rs`

## A duplicate

- The research also answers whether something covers it. The prompt carries the open pull requests beside the open issues, `fenced` and without the issue itself: number, title, and the issues its body names with `Resolves`, `Fixes` or `Closes`, from `Forge::open_pulls`: the keyword is a word of its own, takes a colon before the number and markup before itself, and a number ends at whitespace or punctuation. The reply gains `addressed_by`: null or `{number, kind, why}`, `kind` being `issue` or `pull_request`. `runner/triage/`, `adapters/gh/issues.rs`, `agents/triager.md`
- Code keeps it only when its number is one the call was shown, the forge still lists it as open and of the kind it was given, and it is not the issue itself. A `kind` that is neither word is dropped and told in the log, and the issue is triaged as any other. An unreadable listing fails the pass. The cover is read again before the post, since wording can take minutes: a cover that went is not posted, and the issue is triaged at the next poll. `runner/triage/addressed.rs`
- A kept one ends the pass: the representative words a short comment naming the other item as `#n` and the reason, one plain thanks naming their report; code appends the marker, `Voice::post` posts it, the record saves `closed`, and then `Tickets::close_issue_as` closes it for reason `duplicate`. `close_issue` is unchanged; no label goes on a closed duplicate. `runner/triage/comment.rs`, `crates/core/src/ports/tickets.rs`, `crates/core/src/ports/issue.rs`
- A restart at `closed` closes it and posts nothing again. `status` says `closed as a duplicate of #n` once the close is done and `to close as a duplicate of #n` while it is owed. An author who reopens one is triaged again only through `shep kelpie triage <n>`.

## The maintainer's issue, rewritten

- `body` is in the issue skill's format: the call loads `kelpie-issue` (`Skills::issue_plugin`), which `agents/triager.md` names rather than repeat. A question with a good answer is `Assumed: <answer>. Say otherwise.` in it, `open` the rest. The reply's `kind`, one of `check::kinds`, goes on as `kind:<type>`, made if missing, unless a `kind:` label is on. `runner/triage/{research,rewrite}.rs`
- With no open question and a `body`, `readiness` rewrites the maintainer's issue (`Runner::maintainer()`, ignoring case). If the check passes the listed title, the body and the labels the pass began with as its own change them (`passes_with`), the body goes over the issue's on the ready path below. If not, it goes under the issue's text as settled answers do (`settled_answers`, `answers`, `commented`, the labels, `needs-triage`), never over it, and the re-check says what was refused. An outside issue is never made ready. `runner/triage/ready.rs`
- The comment says ready, with the priority, or, for a rewrite, that the check still refuses it. It is promised only where kelpie can keep it: no hand triage label stands, and the local guard passes the body. `facts.rs`
- `Voice::edit_issue_body` replaces the body, the title untouched: `AppVoice` sends `PATCH /repos/{owner}/{repo}/issues/{number}` naming only `body`, through `GuardedVoice`, `PacedVoice`, `RecordedVoice`. A body naming a path on this machine is dropped before the wording (`LocalPaths`), and the issue ends at `needs-triage` as written. A `publish` hook's refusal is said on the issue in a short notice. `ports/voice.rs`, `github/voice/`
- On the ready path the comment is followed by the body edit (`body-written`), the labels, the removal of an earlier pass's `needs-triage`, and `ready-for-agent` last (`ready`), made if missing. The `labelled` step is not used there.
- A refused edit is told once and tried at the next poll. Once `body-written` is saved the body is never written again: `ready_body` holds it at `commented`.
- A triage label put on by hand stands. The labels the issue began its pass with (the record's `seen`, kept over a restart) are read against the issue before the wording, the body write, the labelling and `needs-triage`, so one that came by ends the pass at `done`, body kept, nothing more labelled. At the labelling step `ready-for-agent` is kelpie's own, and the step finishes. `ready.rs`
- `ready` counts as finished: pruned like `done`, no new pass. The board takes it as any `ready-for-agent` issue, its `agent:` label as ever.
