# The author's round

An open question, one the triager has no good answer for (`triage-check.md`), goes to the issue's author before it goes to the maintainer (#887, #892, #893, #1108): one round, answered in a comment on GitHub or in the author's own terminal, and `needs-approval` for what is left. The comment that hands an outside issue over mentions the maintainer, which `triage-reader.md` has. The local prompt and the body rewrite are in `triage-local.md`. The settings, the pick rule, the verb and `status` are in `triage.md`; the pass, its post and its labels are in `triage-pass.md`; hearing the reply, how it confirms and how the round ends in silence are in `triage-answers.md`.

- `Runner::asked_login` is who is asked and whose reply answers, in the comment and in what a re-ask reads back: the maintainer when the author is the project's own App, else the author. `Runner::askable` is whether it names anyone: the maintainer on their own issue, and any author under `pick = "all"`; an App issue with no maintainer login ends at `needs-approval` as an unasked one does. `triage_tries.author` and `Triaged.author` stay the issue's author, which `limits.rs` counts by. `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- Asked and not sure: one comment holds the representative's lead-in, the questions and, below them, the local prompt inside a collapsed `<details>` section. No status label goes on while the triage waits. `Runner::finish_triage` returns at a record whose `questions` or `local` are open, and the type and priority the pass owed wait in its `labels`. `Runner::ask_comment` builds the body, with a `cc @<login>` line under the words when the asked login is not the author, `runner/triage/comment.rs`, `runner/triage/local.rs`
- `Runner::author_role` (`runner/triage/ask.rs`) is what an author may be asked, tried in this order: the role `triage.authors` lists for their login, `maintainer` for `Runner::maintainer()`, else the one their association implies (`AuthorRole::of`: `OWNER` maintainer, `MEMBER`, `COLLABORATOR` and `CONTRIBUTOR` developer, else reporter). The role fixes the call's question cap (`AuthorRole::most_questions`: `max_questions` for a maintainer, at most 3 for a developer, 1 for a reporter, never past `max_questions`), which `read_reply` enforces on the reply, and the call prompt names the association and the role (`research::prompt`). `agents/triager.md` sets what each may be asked. For any role but `maintainer` the files, tests and criteria stay out of the facts the representative words from and out of the local prompt, so nothing posted to them names a path. A `P0` is asked of a maintainer first, as today; for any other role it is asked last, as "How urgent is this for you?" with no label named, so the cap drops it before the triager's own questions. A pass that lost its role to a restart is worded as for a reporter. #1109
- The questions are kelpie's code, not the representative's words: the triager's `open`, at most `max_questions`, numbered, each with its recommended answer, in the vendored grilling skill's shape (`skills/skills/productivity/grilling`) without its bold. A backtick or tilde fence in a question or answer is dropped, so it cannot swallow the section below. `questions_block`, `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- `triage.issues` gains `questions {comment, at}` (state version 67) and `local {at, body}` (state version 68), each left out while unset. Both are set when the comment goes up, and either ends the wait. `at` is when the comment went up and is what silence is measured from. A record made from the marker read asks the comment itself what it owes: one holding the questions block saves `questions` with `at` from that comment's own created time, so an author who answered while the post was still being read back is heard, and one with no questions block records `done` (`holds_questions`, `Marked`, `runner/triage/{ask,marker}.rs`). `asked {at, asks}` is no longer written. A file saved on it still loads, and `answers_wait` reads its wait as the answers wait on the triage comment. `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- The triager writes a criterion only from what the issue or the code makes certain. A flag, a message's wording, a status, which cases count or a default is an `open` question with its recommended answer, and `body` carries each answer's exact wording once answered. The cap stays `max_questions`, the weightiest question first. `agents/triager.md`
