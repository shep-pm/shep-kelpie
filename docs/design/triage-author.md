# The author's round

An open question, one the triager has no good answer for (`triage-check.md`), goes to the issue's author before it goes to the maintainer (#887, #892, #893, #1108): one round, answered in a comment on GitHub or in the author's own terminal, and `needs-triage` for what is left. The comment that hands an outside issue over mentions the maintainer, which `triage-reader.md` has. The local prompt and the body rewrite are in `triage-local.md`. The settings, the pick rule, the verb and `status` are in `triage.md`; the pass, its post and its labels are in `triage-pass.md`.

- `Runner::asked_login` is who is asked and whose reply answers, in the comment and in what a re-ask reads back: the maintainer when the author is the project's own App, else the author. `Runner::askable` is whether it names anyone: the maintainer on their own issue, and any author under `pick = "all"`; an App issue with no maintainer login ends at `needs-triage` as an unasked one does. `triage_tries.author` and `Triaged.author` stay the issue's author, which `limits.rs` counts by. `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- Asked and not sure: one comment holds the representative's lead-in, the questions and, below them, the local prompt inside a collapsed `<details>` section. No status label goes on while the triage waits. `Runner::finish_triage` returns at a record whose `questions` or `local` are open, and the type and priority the pass owed wait in its `labels`. `Runner::ask_comment` builds the body, with a `cc @<login>` line under the words when the asked login is not the author, `runner/triage/comment.rs`, `runner/triage/local.rs`
- `Runner::author_role` (`runner/triage/ask.rs`) is what an author may be asked, tried in this order: the role `triage.authors` lists for their login, `maintainer` for `Runner::maintainer()`, else the one their association implies (`AuthorRole::of`: `OWNER` maintainer, `MEMBER`, `COLLABORATOR` and `CONTRIBUTOR` developer, else reporter). The role fixes the call's question cap (`AuthorRole::most_questions`: `max_questions` for a maintainer, at most 3 for a developer, 1 for a reporter, never past `max_questions`), which `read_reply` enforces on the reply, and the call prompt names the association and the role (`research::prompt`). `agents/triager.md` sets what each may be asked. For any role but `maintainer` the files, tests and criteria stay out of the facts the representative words from and out of the local prompt, so nothing posted to them names a path. A `P0` is asked of a maintainer first, as today; for any other role it is asked last, as "How urgent is this for you?" with no label named, so the cap drops it before the triager's own questions. A pass that lost its role to a restart is worded as for a reporter. #1109
- The questions are kelpie's code, not the representative's words: the triager's `open`, at most `max_questions`, numbered, each with its recommended answer, in the vendored grilling skill's shape (`skills/skills/productivity/grilling`) without its bold. A backtick or tilde fence in a question or answer is dropped, so it cannot swallow the section below. `questions_block`, `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- `triage.issues` gains `questions {comment, at}` (state version 67) and `local {at, body}` (state version 68), each left out while unset. Both are set when the comment goes up, and either ends the wait. `at` is when the comment went up and is what silence is measured from. A record made from the marker read asks the comment itself what it owes: one holding the questions block saves `questions` with `at` from that comment's own created time, so an author who answered while the post was still being read back is heard, and one with no questions block records `done` (`holds_questions`, `Marked`, `runner/triage/{ask,marker}.rs`). `asked {at, asks}` is no longer written. A file saved on it still loads, and `answers_wait` reads its wait as the answers wait on the triage comment. `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- Hearing: `read_threads` reads while any triage waits, `Runner::triage_waits` putting each wait's start into the read's `since` and holding the read's floor there while a wait is open, so a busy forge or a held slot does not drop the reply, and `Runner::hear_triage` keeps only what the asked login wrote on that issue after the comment went up, by login through `plain_login`. A bot, the App's own post and anyone else's words are dropped. The login is the pass's, taken from the open listing each poll so a restart has it. With no login to ask, only silence ends the wait. `runner/thread_replies/`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
- Any comment from the asked login is their answers, whatever it says: no word is read from it. One that opens with `github` or `local`, or answers only some questions, still starts the second pass, which reads it against the questions and asks again only what it left unsettled. `runner/representative/reading.rs` is no longer used by triage.
- How a reply confirms (#1177): the ask comment ends its questions with `REPLY_HINT`, saying `ok` takes every recommended answer and `Qn: ...` changes one. `answers_round` expands the reply before the second pass reads it: a line that is only `ok` (any case, a trailing `.` or `!` allowed) becomes `Qn: <recommended>` for every question, each `Qn: ...` line, with the lines under it, replaces that one, and any other line is kept after them. A reply with no `ok` line goes as written, and a question it leaves out stays unanswered for the pass to ask again. The recommended answers are read back from the questions comment (`recommended_in`), so a restart that lost the held questions still expands `ok`. `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- The triager writes a criterion only from what the issue or the code makes certain. A flag, a message's wording, a status, which cases count or a default is an `open` question with its recommended answer, and `body` carries each answer's exact wording once answered. The cap stays `max_questions`, the weightiest question first. `agents/triager.md`
- The author's comments after the triage comment are the answers. A second triager call (`Purpose::Answers`) reads the issue, the questions and the answers, each fenced, and answers again. The maintainer's own issue is rewritten over only where the issue check passes the rewrite, which makes it ready, and otherwise as an outside issue is (`triage-pass.md`). An outside issue gets what is settled written under its author's own text in its body, never in place of it: the `answers` step, with the whole body in `ready_body`. `Runner::settled_answers`, `Runner::answers_write`. What is still open is listed in that comment, and there is no second round. A body the forge cannot be read for is not stood for by a comment and labels: the pass runs again. A write a hook refused keeps the type and priority the pass found.
- No comment from the author `triage.wait_days` after the triage comment: the labels the pass owed go on, then `needs-triage`, and nothing is posted, so the triage comment stays the maintainer's starting point. No second ask is posted. `Runner::triage_none`
- A triage label put on by hand, or the issue closed, ends the wait with no post and no label: `Runner::settle_triage_waits` reads the listing each poll, and `Runner::triage_wait_stood` saves the record `done` with nothing owed. Only `needs-triage` joins the labels the pass began with at the moment of recording, whatever listing the pass read: it is the queue label and not a decision, so one the issue already carried is not mistaken for one put on during the wait. No label that says what to do with the issue is joined, so one put on by hand while the pass was wording its comment stands, and ends the wait (#1276, #1304).
- The wait holds no slot and no call of its own. The call it starts is taken from `Runner::triage_round_due`, after the same pick as the first, and takes a slot as the first does.
- No wait is settled, read or asked for under a triager call in flight on the same issue, and a given-up issue is neither heard from nor settled until kelpie restarts. `Runner::answers_round` returning none is no call. `runner/triage/ask.rs`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
