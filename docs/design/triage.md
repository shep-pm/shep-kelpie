# Triage

Kelpie's triage of issues other people open (#887): the settings, the agent, the pick rule, the verb and `status`. The issue check that owes an issue its triage is in `triage-check.md`, the limits in `triage-limits.md`, one issue's pass, its duplicate close and its agent-ready branch are in `triage-pass.md`, the author's round, its ask and its own session in `triage-author.md`, the classifier check in `triage-spam.md`, and the take-back of hand-labelled issues in `triage-hand.md`.

- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage]`, an absent table meaning `pick = "off"`, with `deny_unknown_fields`. `crates/core/src/settings/triage.rs`
  - `pick`: `off` (default), `maintainer` (only the maintainer's issues) or `all`
  - `hand_ready`: bool, default `false`, refused by `Triage::check` naming `triage.hand_ready` while `pick` is `off`; the take-back is the pick rule's, below
  - `wait_days`: whole days above 0, default 7
  - `max_questions`: 1 to 10, default 10, refused above 10 by `Triage::check`.
  - `per_day` (default 10), `per_author_per_day` (default 2), `min_account_days` (default 30) and `max_body_kb` (default 20): each `0` for off, a negative or non-number value refused by name. `crates/core/src/settings/tests/triage.rs`
  - `authors`: login to `AuthorRole` (`maintainer`, `developer`, `reporter`), logins compared ignoring case, absent meaning none, an unknown word refused by name. `AuthorRole` and `Triage::role_of`, `crates/core/src/settings/triage.rs`. `triage-author.md` says what a role changes
  - `agent`: an agent file whose role is `triager`, default `triager`. `Settings::triager` checks it.
- `[app.dogs.kelpie.triage.spam]`, absent meaning no check: `gateway`, `path` and `threshold` (default 0.8, refused outside 0 to 1). `triage-spam.md`
- `reread` takes a changed `triage` at once: it runs `open_checks::triage` on the new settings and agent book, refuses the change as a start does, and then calls `settle_since`, saving the state when it changed. A refused change keeps the old settings. Any change of `pick` drops the in-memory `triage.due`, which the next poll fills under the new rule, and `off` also drops the verb's queue and ends the call in flight, whose reply is discarded once `state.triage` is `None` (`runner/flight/triage.rs`). `runner/reread/`
- Agent file `triager.md`, role `triager`, `Expert` frontmatter, shipped on Claude Code and written by `add`. It researches an issue against a clean checkout and reports in one JSON object: `type`, `kind`, `priority`, `related`, `addressed_by`, `files`, `tests`, `criteria`, `open` and `body`, the last the issue in the issue skill's format. The issue text is data. `agents/triager.md`
- Every harness gives the triager the reviewer's read-only reach (`Tools::Review`), per `harness.triager` in `docs/parity.toml`.
- `Role::Triager` in `crates/core/src/agents/mod.rs` and `crate::ports::Role`. No work item records its calls (`crates/core/src/work_item/{spend,tally}.rs`). The code graph reaches it when on (`reads_code`, `runner/code_graph/`), with its own index (`ProjectPaths::triage_graph`), removed when the call ends. `CallKind::Triage` gives it a `triager` total in `usage`, apart from work items. `crates/core/src/usage/mod.rs`
- `Runner::open` checks the settings in `runner/open_checks.rs`: while `triage.pick` is not `off` the App must cover the repo (as `contributions.pick` does), and `triage.agent` and `agents.representative` must name files with the right role; under `off` none is read.
- `OpenIssue` carries `author`, `author_is_bot`, `author_association`, `labels` and `created`. `author_association` is how the forge counts whoever opened it to the repo (`AuthorAssociation`), answered only by the REST listing: gh 2.96 refuses `authorAssociation` from `gh issue list --json` (`docs/facts/what-ghs-listings-answer.md`). REST answers pull requests beside issues and no filter that separates them, so its pages are read until 1000 issues are in hand or it runs out, bounded at `LIST_PAGES` calls each cut at 1000 keeping the newest. `crates/core/src/ports/issue.rs`, `adapters/gh/issues.rs`. `Forge::account_created(login)` reads when an account was made.
- `ProjectState.triage { since }` (version 60) is when `pick` went past `off`, left out while unset. Only an issue created after it is triaged, but a held `ready-for-agent` one: no reach back over the backlog. `Runner::open` and `reread` set it, when `pick` is not `off` and none is saved, and clear it under `off`, so it is `None` only while `pick` is `off`. A change between `maintainer` and `all` keeps it. `pruned_through` (version 76) is the highest issue number a prune dropped a record of, left out while 0. A file saved before it that holds `TRIAGED_KEPT` finished records loads with the highest recorded issue as the floor, since a prune may have run. `runner/triage/` (`settle_since`), `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- A triage record carries `at` (when it was made), `author`, `held` (the limit that held it) and `calls`, the moment of every triager call its issue made, oldest first, saved as each ends, the last `TRIAGE_CALLS_KEPT` kept (state version 70). A call counts once it reached a model. A record a limit held, and one made from a triage comment found on the issue, hold no call. A record saved before them reads as none of the three, so it counts toward none. A call on an issue with no record yet (a first pass leaves one until its comment posts), is saved under `triage.unrecorded` with its author, which a record then takes. `TriageState::prune` keeps a record holding a call ahead of one holding none, so a burst of holds cannot push the counted records out. `crates/core/src/state/triage.rs`
- The pick rule runs once a board poll while `pick` is not `off`, over `open_issues`. An issue is untouched when the check refused it, it is open and carries none of `TRIAGE_LABELS` but `ready-for-agent` and `needs-triage` above `pruned_through`, no other bot than the App opened it, it has no record (or a `taken` one, or a finished one edited since), it came after `triage.since` or carries (or was taken back from) `ready-for-agent`, and under `maintainer` `Runner::maintainer()` (ignoring case) or the App opened it.
- `triage.hand_ready` (#1179) takes back the maintainer's hand-labelled `ready-for-agent` issues for every worker, in `apply_poll` before the board's pick. `triage-hand.md`
- `status` lists them under `triage.due`, oldest first (number, title, author, author_association, created), left out when none; plain `status` adds a `triage due` line. The list lives in memory, rebuilt by the next poll. `runner/triage/`, `runner/trigger/`, `src/flock/control/plain.rs`
- `status` says the day's cap as `triage.daily` (`used`, `cap`, `opens`, `wait`) while triage and the cap are on, and plain `status` says the count and when the next place opens. A held record is said in the plain block in `TriageHeld::said`'s words.
- A listing that cannot be read, or a maintainer that cannot be named, fails no poll.
- `shep kelpie triage <n>` queues `n` ahead of `triage.due`, past `since` and the author test. Refused when `pick` is `off`, when `n` is not open or carries a triage label but `needs-triage`, or when its call is in flight. A recorded issue is triaged again, its record replaced when the new comment posts. A pass asked again is given the rounds it ran: the App's own triage comments on the issue and their author's replies, fenced and oldest first, and a question an earlier round asked and its author answered is dropped from what the new pass may post. Each round's replies end where the next questions comment went up, and a reply settles a round's questions by a line that is only `ok` (all of them) or that question's `Qn:` line (that one). A reply in neither form settles none that code can name: the triager reads it. A question is compared as `ask::questions_block` wrote it, first line and one case, so what was read back off a comment equals what the triager named. `Runner::earlier_rounds`, `asked_in`, `ask::answers_one`.
- `status` lists the call in flight under `triage.running` and the records under `triage.issues`, a closed one with its `duplicate_of`.
