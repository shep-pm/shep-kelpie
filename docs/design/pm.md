# Project manager

The agent kelpie wakes to pick, answer and unstick. The board file it reads is in pm-board.md. Its batch retro is in [pm-retro.md](pm-retro.md). ADR 0020 gives it the calls, and shrinks settings to the agent files, `rulings.pm_decides` and a fact kelpie cannot detect: `docs/adr/0020-the-project-manager-makes-the-calls.md`.

- `agents.pm` opts a project in (`pm`, Opus 5.5 at medium; Sonnet cost about the same here); without one board rule picks and every ruling waits on maintainer. `runner/pm/`, #327
- Code wakes it, never a timer: free slot with 2+ choices (told how many slots are free), a head red on `main` after its rerun (once per head, and once more if its fix item closes while it stays red, at once, whatever the ready count), new conflict (a clash that is not `main`'s), stuck item, a lease wait past 10 min, worker question, `shep kelpie tell`. A stuck item, a conflict and a lease wait are reasons nobody waits on, so each holds 3 min and the ones that arrive inside it go in one wake; a free slot, a tell, a question, a follow-up, a ruling to answer or a pick ends the hold at once, carrying them. What the board no longer shows is dropped before the call starts, whatever ends the window, and nothing runs while nothing is left. A reason already queued takes the state the board shows now, and a wake whose session cannot resume starts a new one at once, its reasons held no second time. #327, #964, #987
- An item it answered `none` for is not woken for again while it is in the same state: parked on a ruling, until that ruling is answered; an idle call, for 30 min, then 60, then 2h at most. A new turn, phase or ruling is a new state; new output in the same turn is not (the 10 min idle flapping). When the hold ends the wake goes out again even if the board showed nothing more, once per hold, so a worker hung during a hold is not left unattended until its turn ceiling ends it. Any other action on the item, or its closing, clears it. #986, `runner/pm/wakes.rs`
- Reads project worktrees + each item's diff (messages alone miss defects); secret-shaped files and kelpie secrets stay denied. #413, `runner/pm/call.rs`
- A workflow red on `main` is the PM's to clear, not a reason to wait. Kelpie writes the failed log of each red head to `ci/main-<run>.log`, and the board's `## Red on main` says what is being done. Once a head is red after kelpie's rerun the `red` key (an object or a list) opens a fix work item (`fix`: `title`, `body` naming the check, log lines and suspect merge, in the issue format; filed `ready-for-agent`, put at the front of the board's order; checked first with `board::check::check`, a stand-in priority and kind standing for the labels a triage pass puts on, and a body that fails is put to the maintainer as an `ask` carrying the draft and each failure, never filed) or asks the maintainer (`ask`: the `log` lines it read, in its `reply`). Each head wakes it once and is settled once, except that a fix work item that closes while its workflow stays red settles it again and wakes it once more, and a new head under a fix work item still open is not woken for (`main-ci.md`). A call before the rerun came back red is dropped. A pick of none while a head is red after its rerun with no call is dropped and the board's rule picks, so a red main with free slots never starts nothing without a rerun, a fix or a question; it may still pick issues clear of the failing area and says why. #1183, `runner/pm/red.rs`, `runner/pm/ci_logs.rs`
- Its notes files: `pm-standing.md` holds the rules that hold across work items, each carrying its end at the line's end (`(until #12)` for when a ticket closes, `(until 2026-10-05)` for after a day), and `pm-notes.md` the state of one work item. Kelpie writes the standing notes and ends every line whose end has passed. The log, its archive and the reads behind an end: pm-notes.md
- No git or gh: kelpie writes diffs (64 KiB each) + a `still-red` item's CI log. #452. The CI logs (`failed_run_logs` per still-red item, `run_log_tail` per red workflow) are read in one job with the runner's lock let go, and the files are written under it from the result (#1210, `runner-lock.md`). A wake from `pm_ended` or `shep kelpie pm` reads them in place.
- Answer is one JSON object (`pick`, `hold`, `unstick`, `answer`, `follow_up`, `reply`) checked against the board; anything else dropped + logged. #327
- What it may pick, hold and set on a pick, and how a pick is read: [pm-pick.md](pm-pick.md)
- One session resumed per wake, compacted past 100k; failed call passed over 10 min, board rule picks meanwhile. #327
- Answers or escalates worker questions, holding the ruling from maintainer one wake (10 min max). #437
- Decides `follow-up` rulings, filing `ready-for-agent` or `needs-triage`. #510, #511
- A ruling the maintainer answers wakes it only on their `<id> pm recommend`, `pm options` or `pm decide`: `who-answers.md`
- `shep kelpie pm` hands its session to maintainer's terminal like `attach`; no wake runs under them. #327
- The digest says how many of its lists it leaves out: the count of known gaps past the newest 40 is given, in the retro's prompt and in the across-batch line, and each work item's findings header carries its total. The gaps past the limit are counted, never named. #1719, `runner/retro/`
- A finding's digest round and its `(late)` tag count rounds read for that work item, in journal order up to the finding, even when a new pass restarts round numbers: the third read and beyond are late. Passed-over reviewers do not count as reads. Round lines keep the journal's numbers, including passed-over reviewers; the journal's format is unchanged. #1712, `runner/retro/digest/`
