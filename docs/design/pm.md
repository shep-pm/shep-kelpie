# Project manager

The agent kelpie wakes to pick, answer and unstick, and the board file it reads.

- Runner writes `board.md` so PM never runs git or gh (code-written board decides as well). Keeps newest 50 events + PM cursor; issue bodies quoted (600 chars) behind `> `; text naming this machine withheld. #326, `runner/briefing/`
- Each wake carries the whole board in its prompt, under a heading after the reasons, and `board.md` holds the same text. The whole board, not a delta: relative ages ("opened 8h46m ago") change on every item line between wakes, so a board diff is noise, and the events section is already the delta. A board past 64 KiB is left out and the Read instruction stays, since the prompt is one argv argument. #989, `runner/pm/prompt.rs`
- `board.md` has an Agents section after the ready queue: the implementers the project lists, the default first and marked, then its reviewers in their order, each with its tier, its kinds (`every kind` when the file sets none) and its description (`no description` when it has none). It reads each `Agent.fit` from the book at each write, so an edit to a file shows at the next one, and no model name reaches it. Shown, not used: nothing picks by the keys yet. #1378, #1425, `core/board/briefing/`, `runner/briefing/`
- Worker silent 10 min reads as idle (PM told only "busy" misses a hung worker). #326
- A call that has not had its gateway lease is never idle or stuck: quiet counts from the grant, and a wait past 10 min is its own wake, not a stuck item. Only a wait for a lease names the lease holder; a gateway that answered busy named no client as the cause, and the board names what the call waits for, and what it got, in the same words the wake uses. #964, `runner/briefing/`, `runner/flight/`
- Overlap from `git diff` + `git merge-tree` (git 2.38+), cached by commit, run without runner lock (trigger never waits on git). A head conflicting with `main` is a `Behind main` line, and those files are out of its pairs. #326, #1318
- `agents.pm` opts a project in (`pm`, Opus 5.5 at medium; Sonnet cost about the same here); without one board rule picks and every ruling waits on maintainer. `runner/pm/`, #327
- Code wakes it, never a timer: free slot with 2+ choices (told how many slots are free), a head red on `main` after its rerun (once per head, at once, whatever the ready count), new conflict (a clash that is not `main`'s), stuck item, a lease wait past 10 min, worker question, `shep kelpie tell`. A stuck item, a conflict and a lease wait are reasons nobody waits on, so each holds 3 min and the ones that arrive inside it go in one wake; a free slot, a tell, a question, a follow-up, a ruling to answer or a pick ends the hold at once, carrying them. What the board no longer shows is dropped before the call starts, whatever ends the window, and nothing runs while nothing is left. A reason already queued takes the state the board shows now, and a wake whose session cannot resume starts a new one at once, its reasons held no second time. #327, #964, #987
- An item it answered `none` for is not woken for again while it is in the same state: parked on a ruling, until that ruling is answered; an idle call, for 30 min, then 60, then 2h at most. A new turn, phase or ruling is a new state; new output in the same turn is not (the 10 min idle flapping). When the hold ends the wake goes out again even if the board showed nothing more, once per hold, so a worker hung during a hold is not left unattended until its turn ceiling ends it. Any other action on the item, or its closing, clears it. #986, `runner/pm/wakes.rs`
- Reads project worktrees + each item's diff (messages alone miss defects); secret-shaped files and kelpie secrets stay denied. #413, `runner/pm/call.rs`
- A workflow red on `main` is the PM's to clear, not a reason to wait. Kelpie writes the failed log of each red head to `ci/main-<run>.log`, and the board's `## Red on main` says what is being done. Once a head is red after kelpie's rerun the `red` key (an object or a list) opens a fix work item (`fix`: `title`, `body` naming the check, log lines and suspect merge; filed `ready-for-agent`, put at the front of the board's order) or asks the maintainer (`ask`: the `log` lines it read, in its `reply`). Each head wakes it once and is settled once, and a call before the rerun came back red is dropped. A pick of none while a head is red after its rerun with no call is dropped and the board's rule picks, so a red main with free slots never starts nothing without a rerun, a fix or a question; it may still pick issues clear of the failing area and says why. #1183, `runner/pm/red.rs`, `runner/pm/ci_logs.rs`
- No git or gh: kelpie writes diffs (64 KiB each) + a `still-red` item's CI log. #452. The CI logs (`failed_run_logs` per still-red item, `run_log_tail` per red workflow) are read in one job with the runner's lock let go, and the files are written under it from the result (#1210, `runner-lock.md`). A wake from `pm_ended` or `shep kelpie pm` reads them in place.
- Tools `Read,Glob,Grep,Edit,Write`; writes only to the end of `pm-notes.md` via `kelpie confine --append`. Kelpie rolls that log past 32 KiB into `pm-archive.md` at a wake, and keeps what a later wake must remember in `pm-standing.md`, which only kelpie writes and asks every wake to mine the archive into until a `standing` answer lands. #327, #988, #1365
- Kelpie appends one line to `pm-notes.md` for every wake it answered: the time, what it woke for, what it did, and the answer's `why`, each on one line, cut at 300 characters, with text naming this machine withheld as on the board. An answer it gave, or one it owed, on a ruling left to it is recorded among what it did, since a wake for a ruling alone has no other action. A wake that failed or gave no readable answer appends nothing, and each tell it carried goes to the standing notes verbatim in the same step. The PM adds only what a later wake needs and that line does not say, and a resumed wake is not sent back to reread its notes. #989, `runner/pm/notes.rs`
- Answer is one JSON object (`pick`, `hold`, `unstick`, `answer`, `follow_up`, `reply`) checked against the board; anything else dropped + logged. #327
- Pick of none lasts 30 min with nothing open. A hold is `{ issue, until }`, `until` an open work item (else refused with a note): it ends when that item closes or the issue's title or body changes, so the PM need not hold again each wake; an answer with no `hold` keeps them, `[]` clears. A bare issue number has no `until` and ends at any close. #327, #1321
- `pick` is one ticket or a list in the order to start, up to the free slots the wake told (extras are dropped and named). Dispatch opens one item a step, taking the next of the list through the usual overlap and hold checks, so a wake fills every free slot. An entry keeps its place until its work item opens; an entry the board cannot take is dropped like a single pick and the next goes. #1223
- A pick of an issue stands whatever opens or closes. Dispatch drops it only where the issue is no longer one the board can take, which logs a `pick-dropped` step with why and tells the PM at its next wake. #1222
- One session resumed per wake, compacted past 100k; failed call passed over 10 min, board rule picks meanwhile. #327
- Answers or escalates worker questions, holding the ruling from maintainer one wake (10 min max). #437
- Decides `follow-up` rulings, filing `ready-for-agent` or `needs-triage`. #510, #511
- A ruling the maintainer answers wakes it only on their `<id> pm recommend`, `pm options` or `pm decide`: `who-answers.md`
- `shep kelpie pm` hands its session to maintainer's terminal like `attach`; no wake runs under them. #327
- Retro moved from worker to PM: batch after every 10 finished items and at a drain, one call with the PM's agent over a digest (`journal.jsonl`, rulings, ledger). Worker keeps a PR-body `Friction` section. Off unless `skills.retro`; no slot, never retried, saved unread (agents acting on retros loop). Digest counts conflicts per file, rounds, late findings, known flakes (also in CI fix prompts). #361, #566, #657, `runner/retro/`
- The digest counts a round that read the pull request and a reviewer passed over, each numbered with its reason; a wake belongs to an item only where the PM woke for it, picked it or acted on it, never where its id sits in a holds list, and `starts nothing now` is a wake that changed nothing; an item's entries cover its own run, so a revived issue is two runs; a first retro's totals start at its batch's first item; an `unpriced` call reads `local, unpriced` and a killed turn `timed out, usage not read`, never `$0.00`. #1316
