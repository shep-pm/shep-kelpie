# Project manager

The agent kelpie wakes to pick, answer and unstick. Its board file: pm-board.md. Its batch retro: pm-retro.md. ADR 0020 gives it the calls, and shrinks settings to the agent files, `rulings.pm_decides` and a fact kelpie cannot detect: `docs/adr/0020-the-project-manager-makes-the-calls.md`.

- `agents.pm` opts a project in (`pm`, a baseline that binds nothing, so its own file binds it); without one board rule picks and every ruling waits on maintainer. `runner/pm/`, #327
- Code wakes it, never a timer: free slot with 2+ choices (told how many slots are free), a head red on `main` after its rerun (once per head, and once more if its fix item closes while it stays red, at once, whatever the ready count), new conflict (a clash that is not `main`'s), stuck item, a lease wait past 10 min, worker question, `shep kelpie tell`. A stuck item, a conflict and a lease wait are reasons nobody waits on, so each holds 3 min and the ones that arrive inside it go in one wake; a free slot, a tell, a question, a follow-up, a ruling to answer or a pick ends the hold at once, carrying them. What the board no longer shows is dropped before the call starts, whatever ends the window, and nothing runs while nothing is left. A reason already queued takes the state the board shows now, and a wake whose session cannot resume starts a new one at once, its reasons held no second time. #327, #964, #987
- An item it answered `none` for is not woken for again while it is in the same state: parked on a ruling, until that ruling is answered; an idle call, for 30 min, then 60, then 2h at most. A new turn, phase or ruling is a new state; new output in the same turn is not. When the hold ends the wake goes out again even if the board showed nothing more, once per hold, so a worker hung during a hold is not left unattended until its turn ceiling ends it. Any other action on the item, or its closing, clears it. #986, `runner/pm/wakes.rs`
- Reads a parked item's case before its diff or worktree, and its worktrees and diffs for any other call (messages alone miss defects); secret-shaped files and kelpie secrets stay denied. The case files: pm-board.md. #413, `runner/pm/call.rs`
- A workflow red on `main` is the PM's to clear, not a reason to wait. Kelpie writes the failed log of each red head to `ci/main-<run>.log`, and the board's `## Red on main` says what is being done. Once a head is red after kelpie's rerun the `red` key (an object or a list) opens a fix work item (`fix`: `title`, `body` naming the check, log lines and suspect merge, in the issue format; filed `ready-for-agent`, put at the front of the board's order; checked first with `board::check::check`, which a triage pass would do with its labels, and a body that fails is put to the maintainer as an `ask` carrying the draft and each failure, never filed) or asks the maintainer (`ask`: the `log` lines it read, in its `reply`). Each head is settled once, and a new head under a fix work item still open is not woken for (`main-ci.md`). A call before the rerun came back red is dropped. A pick of none while a head is red after its rerun with no call is dropped and the board's rule picks, so a red main with free slots never starts nothing without a rerun, a fix or a question; it may still pick issues clear of the failing area and says why. #1183, `runner/pm/red.rs`, `runner/pm/ci_logs.rs`
- Its notes files: `pm-standing.md` holds the rules that hold across work items, each carrying its end at the line's end, and `pm-notes.md` the state of one work item. Kelpie writes the standing notes and ends every line whose end has passed. The log, its archive and the reads behind an end: pm-notes.md
- No git or gh: kelpie writes diffs (64 KiB each) + a `still-red` item's CI log. #452. The CI logs (`failed_run_logs` per still-red item, `run_log_tail` per red workflow) are read in one job with the runner's lock let go, and the files are written under it from the result (#1210, `runner-lock.md`).
- It never changes the system: not settings, agent files, kelpie's own code or this machine, and `pm_reach` writes nothing past its folder. A fault there is reported, never worked around: `fix` parks a stuck item on its open fix, or drafts one, which a second call reads against the code first. who-answers.md (#1808, #1810, #1812).
- Answer is one JSON object (`pick`, `hold`, `unstick`, `fix`, `run_log`, `answer`, `follow_up`, `red`, `votes`, `read`, `threads`, `said`, `standing`, `reply`) checked against the board; anything else dropped + logged. #327, `runner/pm/answer/mod.rs`
- A `pick` entry may be an object `{ issue, tier, agent, slack, review }`, `review` an object of `reads` and `about` that a work item keeps as `review_plan` (state version 100). The pick, its holds and how an entry is read: [pm-pick.md](pm-pick.md)
- A round's findings wake it (`Wake::Findings`, queued by `pm_ask_read`) when a pass has every read the plan calls for in and the last read sent a finding above a nit: the wake names the pull request, the reads so far, each finding on one line (20 at most) and what the change touches. No `held` wake, as an item waits on it, so `Wake::same` matches on the ticket. It rules with `read`: `[{ item, more, why }]`. What `more` does to the plan, and what ends the pass without one: `review-more.md`. The ask is in memory (`Desk.reading`, no state-file change), and the item idles in review while it is out; none goes out while the runner drains, the project manager is down or a terminal holds its session. `act` puts each `why` into its `acted` line. ADR 0020, #1764, `runner/pm/more.rs`, `runner/review/one_more.rs`
- An open thread's fate wakes it (`Wake::Threads`, queued by `pm_ask_threads`). It rules with `threads`: `[{ item, thread, decision, why }]`, `decision` `fix`, `follow-up`, `close` or `push-back`. [pm-threads.md](pm-threads.md)
- One session resumed per wake, compacted past 100k; failed call passed over 10 min, board rule picks meanwhile. #327
- Answers each question a wake carried, each in its own `answer` entry (an object or a list) settled in turn, so a dropped one leaves the others standing. It answers with `text` where its case, the code and the docs give an argument, and puts that argument in `why`. It escalates only where a public interface, the project's direction or a cost past the item's budget is at stake, and holds the ruling from the maintainer one wake (10 min max), more while a log it asked for with `run_log` is read (pm-case.md, #1813). A question no entry answers goes to the maintainer after the wake. #437, #1808, #1811
- Decides `follow-up` rulings, filing `ready-for-agent` or `needs-approval`. #510, #511
- A ruling the maintainer answers wakes it only on their `<id> pm recommend`, `pm options` or `pm decide`: `who-answers.md`
- The maintainer's own comment on an open issue with no work item and no triage wait is words to the PM, held as free text under a ruling is: `pm-words.md`. `issue_words` holds it where `agents.pm` is named and the App covers the repo, for the maintainer's own login and never the App's, not a bot, not kelpie's post, on an open issue in the triage poll's listing (issues alone) with no open work item and no triage wait, written at or after this runner's first read (`Reading.first`, in memory, so a restart holds no older comment; `judged` holds each once). Where no listing is known yet, `unheld` keeps the comment for a later read, 15 min at most, then notes it as left unheard. It takes `held_line(thread, id, None, words)`, rides `Wake::Told`, no new wake, and gets a thumbs up; anyone else's is read and dropped. The PM answers with `said` `{thread, call: "none", why}`, posting `why` on the issue; `park`, `send-back` and `settle` are refused there. Cost: one paged `comments_since` per poll where nothing is watched, 12 to 60 an hour by the poll's backoff; `since` moves, so an ETag rarely saves a read; the listing is reused and `can_write` is not called; each held comment costs one reaction and one post. #1848, `runner/thread_replies/issue_words.rs`
- `shep kelpie pm` hands its session to maintainer's terminal like `attach`; no wake runs under them. #327
