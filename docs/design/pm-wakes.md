# The project manager's wakes

What wakes the project manager, the window its reasons wait in, and what kelpie leaves alone. The agent, its answers and its scope: [pm.md](pm.md). Its board: [pm-board.md](pm-board.md).

- Code wakes it, never a timer: free slot with 2+ choices (told how many slots are free), a head red on `main` after its rerun (once per head, and once more if its fix item closes while it stays red, at once, whatever the ready count), new conflict (a clash that is not `main`'s), stuck item, a lease wait past 10 min, worker question, `shep kelpie tell`. A stuck item, a conflict and a lease wait are reasons nobody waits on, so each holds 3 min and the ones that arrive inside it go in one wake; a free slot, a tell, a question, a follow-up, a ruling to answer or a pick ends the hold at once, carrying them. What the board no longer shows is dropped before the call starts, whatever ends the window, and nothing runs while nothing is left. A reason already queued takes the state the board shows now, and a wake whose session cannot resume starts a new one at once, its reasons held no second time. #327, #964, #987
- An item it answered `none` for is not woken for again while it is in the same state: parked on a ruling, until that ruling is answered; an idle call, for 30 min, then 60, then 2h at most. A new turn, phase or ruling is a new state; new output in the same turn is not. When the hold ends the wake goes out again even if the board showed nothing more, once per hold, so a worker hung during a hold is not left unattended until its turn ceiling ends it. Any other action on the item, or its closing, clears it. #986, `runner/pm/wakes.rs`
- A stuck item's ruling its reply named nothing for is asked about once more: its wake is queued again, the next wake is told the reply held no `unstick` for it and that `none` leaves it alone on purpose, and the log names the ruling instead of `nothing`. The reply after that leaves the item alone as `none` would, so no ruling wakes it in a loop. The item is named by `unstick`, `fix` or `answer`, or by a `vote` on its ruling; a ruling answered while its wake was out is not asked about at all. In memory (`Desk.nudged`), no state-file change. #1999, `runner/pm/unanswered.rs`
