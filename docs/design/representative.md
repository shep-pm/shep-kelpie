# Representative

The agent that words what kelpie says to a contributor, in one voice. Code decides what happens and drafts the facts. The representative only puts them into words. Slices 1 and 3 of #616. #627 posts a local reviewer's pass through this call (`contribution-pass.md`), and the slices after it wire the other posts.

- Agent file `representative.md`, role `representative`, `Expert`-shaped frontmatter (harness, model, effort, `usage`, `lease`, `url`, `gateway`, `context`), so it runs on any harness as the triager does. Shipped as Opus 5.5 at low effort on Claude Code, written by `add`. `crates/core/src/agents/mod.rs`, `crates/core/src/agents/front/mod.rs`, #869
- `agents.representative` names it, default `representative`. `Settings::representative` checks it apart from `role_agents`, so `contributions.pick = "off"` needs no file. `crates/core/src/settings/agents/mod.rs`
- `Runner::open` checks it when `contributions.pick` is not `off`. A name with no file, or a file whose role is not `representative`, refuses to start naming `agents.representative`, as `security_reviewer` does. The built-in default stands in for a missing `representative.md`, so only a custom name can lack a file.
- The shipped body speaks as kelpie in the first person, openly a bot that works for the maintainer, never as the maintainer. Warm, humour rare and never in a no, a thanks-close, a second release ask, a frustrated reply or security. It leads with what the contributor does, never decides or promises a merge (a ruling decides), states only the facts given, writes English only (one line in the contributor's language allowed), at most one emoji and none in bad news, no sign-off. Its house rules: no em dashes, bold labels, "not X but Y", one-line closers or staged openers.
- The body's writing guide is `mix-v2`, the text `docs/specs/wording-results.md` ends with, and the effort is low. It sits under the house rules, which win where the two differ. `crates/core/src/agents/tests/representative.rs` pins that the body holds the results file's guide word for word. The test (49 calls, $3.67, experiments repo commit 384c087):
  - Stage 3: `mix-v2` won 6 of 7 blind picks against `mix`, both at low effort, and kept 92% of the facts against 96%. The judge flagged 1 of its posts as inventing after the maintainer voided two flags, against 3 for `mix`. It dropped two dates.
  - Stage 2: low won 4 of 7 against medium on `mix`, with 188 output tokens a post against 297 and 5% less per call. Medium missed the 4 wins rule 3 asks.
  - `mix-v2` cost $0.040 a call and 5,120 input tokens, against $0.118 and 14,577 for `humanizer`.
  - The guide is distilled from `humanizer` (MIT, Siqi Chen), `i-have-adhd` (MIT, Ayoub Ghriss) and the maintainer's own voice. The agent file's comment block carries both MIT notices. `rin-voice` is not shipped, and stays in the maintainer's home copy. Which of it and `mix-v2` reads better there is untested.
- Kelpie's code words time, so the model never works out a weekday or a gap from a date. `Runner::moment_in_words(at)` words a moment against the runner's clock, by the UTC days between: "yesterday", "three days ago" (words up to ten, then "13 days", and weeks, months and years from two weeks out), "today", "tomorrow", "by Sunday (in 4 days)" up to a week ahead, and further out "by Friday 6 November" with the year when it is not this one and no gap, since the guide never gives a date and a relative time together. A moment or a clock past what a date can hold gives `None`, and the prompt then leaves its "Today is" line out rather than state a wrong day. A fact a contribution slice drafts goes through it, never a bare date, since bare dates came back as "by 2024-11-03" or "2024-10-27, three days ago" and the maintainer found both cold. The call's prompt opens with "Today is Wednesday 14 October 2026." from the same clock. `runner/representative/`
- One call per post: `Runner::represent(&Post)` in `runner/representative/`. A fresh session every time (never resumed), `Tools::Answer`, no read folders and no fence, so it reads and runs nothing. It runs in `<project>/representative/` and gets no repo plugins or MCP servers (`repo_setup` does not list it).
- The prompt opens with today's weekday and date, then holds the kind of post (a short instruction from the caller), the facts the caller drafted, the representative's earlier posts and the contributor's text. On an issue the place line also states the issue-post rule, the same one line for every issue kind: greet the author by name and lead with what kelpie found, at most one plain thanks that names what they reported, never a stock "thanks for opening this" or "thanks for the report", and never the opener. Earlier posts come as comment ids and are read again through the forge, so edits show. One that cannot be read is left out. Earlier posts and the contributor's text go through `runner/untrusted.rs` as data.
- The call is ended after 15 minutes, and that is a `Call` error, since nothing else holds a synchronous call. The reply is checked in code before it is returned. Every `@login` (also `@org/team`, `@app[bot]`) that is not the contributor's goes in backticks so it pings nobody. Mentions already in an inline code span with a closing run on its line, or in a fenced block that closes, are left alone, and a backtick or a fence that closes nothing protects nothing (a span is paired within a line, because a list item or heading ends one, and a line of three backticks with another backtick after them is a span, not a fence). An email's `@`, and one in a web address are left alone. A backslash before an `@` protects nothing, since a reader may drop it, and an odd run loses its last backslash so it does not escape the backtick. Nothing is refused for its content.
- A failed call or an empty reply is an error to the caller (`RepresentError`), and the caller's own failure rule applies. The pacer is the caller's too.
- Spend: a ledger line under role `representative`, kind `post`, with the pull request, and a `representative` total in `usage` apart from the work items. No work item records it (`crates/core/src/work_item/tally.rs`, `spend/mod.rs`), and no code graph reaches it.
- Harnesses: Claude Code, pi, Codex and goose all run it (`harness.representative` in `docs/parity.toml`). goose's `refuse_unsupported` allows a `Representative` `Answer` call, which with no fence runs in chat mode with no extension.
