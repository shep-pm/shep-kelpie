# Who a triage comment is for

What a triage comment says of the maintainer depends on whose issue it is (#1144). The label does not change: every pass that is not ready ends at `needs-approval`. The pass and its post are in `triage-pass.md`, the author's round in `triage-author.md` and `triage-answers.md`.

- `Reader` (`runner/triage/facts.rs`) is told to the representative with the facts: `Maintainer` on an issue whose author is `Runner::maintainer()`, ignoring case, `Mentioned` on someone else's hand-over comment, `Other` otherwise.
- The maintainer's issue whose rewrite the check passes (`Wording.ready`): the facts say the issue as kelpie rewrites it passes its issue check, that the body goes over the issue's and `ready-for-agent` goes on, and name the priority the pass put on.
- Every other pass on the maintainer's issue (the ask comment included): the facts tell the representative to speak to them as "you", list what is still theirs to settle (the open questions, and the type, priority or agent-ready body the pass did not reach), give the priority it would recommend, and forbid calling the issue ready or ready to be assigned. How it ends is told by case: `needs-approval`, waiting on them; for the ask comment, no label yet and their answers decide; for a rewrite the check refuses, the body goes under their own text and kelpie says what the check refused; for a triage label they put on by hand while the call ran, that label stands and kelpie labels nothing more. No pass is told it may, since `ready-for-agent` is only ever put on by the ready path. A reply that splits the issue is told its own way instead: the issue is split, each sub-issue labelled `ready-for-agent`, and the parent never ready (`split_facts`).
- Someone else's issue: the comment that hands it over mentions the maintainer. Those are a first pass that asks nothing, the answers pass and the pass that reads a rewritten body. The ask comment, the duplicate close and `triage_none` (which posts nothing) mention no one.
- The mention is one line kelpie's code writes, `cc @<login>`, after the representative's words and before `MARKER`, and the login is `Runner::maintainer()`. The representative's own `@` is put in backticks as ever. The facts say the maintainer is mentioned, so its words promise nothing else.
- The facts head the files, tests and draft criteria as already on the issue, and the kinds name one only where it changes what the reader does next: a comment that lists them again reads as a checklist rather than a reply. `runner/triage/facts.rs`
- The labels line says when they really go on, since a comment cannot point at labels nobody can see: a pass that labels beside its comment says they are on the issue, an ask says they come when the triage ends and are not there yet, and a hand label that stands leaves no labels line at all. `runner/triage/facts.rs`
- When no login can be read, the comment posts without the line and the log says so once a run (`triage_unmentioned_told`, as `unmentioned_told` does for rulings), and the facts do not claim a mention. `runner/triage/comment.rs`
