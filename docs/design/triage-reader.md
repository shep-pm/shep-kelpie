# Who a triage comment is for

What a triage comment says of the maintainer depends on whose issue it is (#1144). The label does not change: every pass that is not ready ends at `needs-approval`. The pass and its post are in `triage-pass.md`, the author's round in `triage-author.md`.

- `Reader` (`runner/triage/facts.rs`) is told to the representative with the facts: `Maintainer` on an issue whose author is `Runner::maintainer()`, ignoring case, `Mentioned` on someone else's hand-over comment, `Other` otherwise.
- The maintainer's issue, sure: the facts say the issue is ready for an agent and name the priority the pass put on.
- The maintainer's issue, not sure (the ask comment included): the facts tell the representative to speak to them as "you", list what is still theirs to settle (the open questions, and the type, priority or agent-ready body the pass did not reach), give the priority it would recommend, and forbid calling the issue ready or ready to be assigned. How it ends is told by case: `needs-approval`, waiting on them; for the ask comment, no label yet and their answers decide; for a triage label they put on by hand while the call ran, that label stands and kelpie labels nothing more. No pass is told it may, since `ready-for-agent` is only ever put on by the ready path.
- Someone else's issue: the comment that hands it over mentions the maintainer. Those are a first pass that asks nothing, the answers pass and the pass that reads a rewritten body. The ask comment, the duplicate close and `triage_none` (which posts nothing) mention no one.
- The mention is one line kelpie's code writes, `cc @<login>`, after the representative's words and before `MARKER`, and the login is `Runner::maintainer()`. The representative's own `@` is put in backticks as ever. The facts say the maintainer is mentioned, so its words promise nothing else.
- When no login can be read, the comment posts without the line and the log says so once a run (`triage_unmentioned_told`, as `unmentioned_told` does for rulings), and the facts do not claim a mention. `runner/triage/comment.rs`
