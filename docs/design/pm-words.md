# The maintainer's words to the project manager

Who answers a ruling is in `who-answers.md`. These are the maintainer's comments that answer none, and the calls the project manager makes on them.

- Free text under a ruling, with `agents.pm`: the maintainer's comment that opens with none of a number, `yes`, `no`, `rework`, `rerun`, `restore` or `pm` is the PM's to read, not a `no <note>`, a worker request or a refusal (ADR 0020, #1759). Under a ruling of `Wants::Merge` or `YesOrNo`, named by number or not, it gets a thumbs up, no "start your reply with its number" and no answer; the ruling waits. A bare conversation comment from `git.maintainer` (else the login `gh` acts as, once known) not under a ruling is held the same way, and a merge ruling is no longer withdrawn by a comment. One written before a ruling's place read is held by the comment round, since its thread never sees it. Words go to `pm_told` as `held_line` (thread, comment, ruling, text), saved with the comment marked heard, and ride `Wake::Told`; `pm_stand_tells` skips them. A question's or a contribution decision's text still answers it, `yes`, `no <note>` and `rework <note>` answer as before, other writers' comments and review threads stay comment rounds, and with no `agents.pm` nothing changes. The PM acts on them with the `said` key (#1760), one `{thread, call, note, why}` per thread, shown on a wake with a held line. `send-back` with a ruling waiting answers it `no <note>` through `Settler::Pm`, posted as a PM answer, so `<id> no <note>` reopens it; with none it releases the comment (`pm.released`, in memory), and the next `talk_turn` sends the worker them. `park` leaves the ruling and says why, or with none raises a `question`. `settle` on a merge ruling with `held_by` accepts those threads, withdraws it and returns the item to CI. A call kelpie cannot carry out (a `no` that makes no worker turn, nothing to settle, a thread with no held line this wake) and held words with no call are named through `inert`, and the ruling and item wait. A merge is no call. Each `why` is in `acted` or `dropped`. `runner/thread_replies/`, `runner/talk/`, `runner/pm/said.rs`, `runner/pm/asked.rs`, `runner/pm/maintainer.rs`
- An issue whose triage asked its author and has not ended is the triage poll's alone (#1959). `apply_threads` notes those issues before `hear_triage`, which may end a lapsed wait in the same read, and `issue_words` judges and holds no comment on them.
- A `none` call with a `why` on a thread with no ruling posts `why` there, whether or not a work item has opened on it since the comment was held. With no `why` it is dropped as "it made no call" and posts nothing. With a ruling it is refused as before. `runner/pm/asked.rs`
