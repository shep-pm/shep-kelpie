# The answers round

How an author's reply is heard, confirmed and answered, and how the round ends in silence (#1177). Who is asked, the roles and the questions comment are in `triage-author.md`; the local prompt and the body rewrite are in `triage-local.md`; the pass, its post and its labels are in `triage-pass.md`; the settings, the pick rule, the verb and `status` are in `triage.md`.

- Hearing: `read_threads` reads while any triage waits, `Runner::triage_waits` putting each wait's start into the read's `since` and holding the read's floor there while a wait is open, so a busy forge or a held slot does not drop the reply, and `Runner::hear_triage` keeps only what the asked login wrote on that issue after the comment went up, by login through `plain_login`. A bot, the App's own post and anyone else's words are dropped. The login is the pass's, taken from the open listing each poll so a restart has it. With no login to ask, only silence ends the wait. `runner/thread_replies/`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
- Any comment from the asked login is their answers: no word is read from it. One that opens with `github` or `local`, or answers only some questions, still starts the second pass, which reads it against the questions and asks again only what it left unsettled. `runner/representative/reading.rs` is no longer used by triage.
- How a reply confirms (#1177): the ask comment ends its questions with `REPLY_HINT`, saying `ok` takes every recommended answer and `Qn: ...` changes one. `answers_round` expands the reply before the second pass reads it: a line that is only `ok` (any case, a trailing `.` or `!` allowed) becomes `Qn: <recommended>` for every question, each `Qn: ...` line, with the lines under it, replaces that one, and any other line is kept after them. A reply with no `ok` line goes as written, and a question it leaves out stays unanswered for the pass to ask again. The recommended answers are read back from the questions comment (`recommended_in`), so a restart that lost the held questions still expands `ok`. `runner/triage/ask.rs`
- The author's comments after the triage comment are the answers. A second triager call (`Purpose::Answers`) reads the issue, the questions and the answers, each fenced, and answers again. Its comment is worded against the answers as the thread holds them, an `ok` before it is expanded, and not against the issue body (`triage-pass.md`). The maintainer's own issue is rewritten over only where the issue check passes the rewrite, which makes it ready, and otherwise as an outside issue is (`triage-pass.md`). An outside issue gets what is settled written under its author's own text in its body, never in place of it: the `answers` step, with the whole body in `ready_body`. `Runner::settled_answers`, `Runner::answers_write`. What is still open is listed in that comment, and there is no second round. A body the forge cannot be read for is not stood for by a comment and labels: the pass runs again. A write a hook refused keeps the type and priority the pass found.
- No comment from the author `triage.wait_days` after the triage comment: the labels the pass owed go on, then `needs-approval`, and nothing is posted, so the triage comment stays the maintainer's starting point. No second ask is posted. `Runner::triage_none`
- A triage label put on by hand, or the issue closed, ends the wait with no post and no label: `Runner::settle_triage_waits` reads the listing each poll, and `Runner::triage_wait_stood` saves the record `done` with nothing owed. Only `needs-approval` joins the labels the pass began with at the moment of recording, whatever listing the pass read: it is the queue label and not a decision, so one the issue already carried is not mistaken for one put on during the wait. No label that says what to do with the issue is joined, so one put on by hand while the pass was wording its comment stands, and ends the wait (#1276, #1304).
- The wait holds no slot and no call of its own. The call it starts is taken from `Runner::triage_round_due`, after the same pick as the first, and takes a slot as the first does.
- No wait is settled, read or asked for under a triager call in flight on the same issue, and a given-up issue is neither heard from nor settled until kelpie restarts. `Runner::answers_round` returning none is no call. `runner/triage/ask.rs`, `runner/triage/heard.rs`
